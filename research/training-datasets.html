Dataset Usage Guidelines

License Information: All datasets are provided under appropriate licenses for research and educational purposes. Please review individual dataset licenses before use.

Recommended Usage

  • Training: Use 70-80% of data for model training
  • Validation: Use 10-15% for hyperparameter tuning
  • Testing: Use 10-15% for final evaluation
  • Cross-validation: Use k-fold validation for robust evaluation

Data Preprocessing

  • Image Augmentation: Apply rotation, scaling, and color jittering
  • Normalization: Standardize pixel values to [0,1] or [-1,1]
  • Resizing: Resize images to consistent dimensions (e.g., 640x640)
  • Annotation Format: Convert to YOLO or COCO format as needed
Important: Always validate your annotations and ensure data quality before training. Poor quality data can significantly impact model performance.
