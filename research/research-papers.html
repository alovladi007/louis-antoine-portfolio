Monte Carlo vs Double Gaussian Models for DUV Lithography Energy Deposition: A Comprehensive Comparison for Semiconductor Manufacturing

L. Antoine
Target: Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B
This work presents a comprehensive comparison between Monte Carlo particle simulation and Double Gaussian analytical models for energy deposition in 193nm deep ultraviolet (DUV) lithography. We evaluate accuracy, computational efficiency, and applicability across different feature sizes (45nm to 7nm nodes) and process conditions. Our study includes statistical validation with 10,000+ simulation runs, uncertainty quantification using bootstrap resampling, and practical implementation guidelines for semiconductor manufacturing applications. The Double Gaussian model demonstrates 15-20% faster computation with 95% accuracy for features >32nm, while Monte Carlo provides superior accuracy for sub-20nm features with 2-3× computational overhead.

Cross-Fab Virtual Metrology and Run-to-Run Control for Advanced Semiconductor Manufacturing: A Machine Learning Approach to Process Optimization

L. Antoine
Target: IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing
This work presents a comprehensive cross-fab virtual metrology and run-to-run (R2R) control system for advanced semiconductor manufacturing, integrating machine learning-based prediction with advanced process control algorithms. The system employs CatBoost gradient boosting for critical dimension (CD) and overlay prediction, conformal prediction for uncertainty quantification, and Double-EWMA control with Kalman filtering for robust process optimization. We demonstrate significant improvements in process stability, yield enhancement, and metrology sampling reduction across 7nm-45nm technology nodes.
