GALILEO V2.0

Geospatial Analytics, Learning, and Intelligence for Land, Environment & Oceanography

Production Ready

Project Overview

GALILEO V2.0 is an enterprise-grade AI-enhanced space-based geophysical sensing platform. A comprehensive, production-ready solution for orbital dynamics, guidance/navigation/control (GNC), geophysical inversion, and machine learning designed for autonomous satellite-based gravimetry missions. Built with JAX for hardware acceleration, featuring complete security/compliance infrastructure, mission trade analysis, and real-time 3D visualization.

Core Capabilities

Orbital Dynamics & Simulation

High-precision orbit propagation with J2, drag, SRP perturbations. Formation flying dynamics and synthetic data generation.

Guidance, Navigation & Control

LQR/LQG/MPC controllers, Extended Kalman Filter navigation, ML-enhanced control with safety systems.

Machine Learning & AI

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN), U-Net for noise reduction, reinforcement learning for autonomous control.

Geophysical Processing

Tikhonov and Bayesian inversion, EGM96/EGM2008 Earth models, seasonal hydrology corrections.

Mission Design & Analysis

1,000+ design configurations, multi-objective optimization, Pareto front identification, risk assessment.

Security & Compliance

Enterprise RBAC, cryptographic audit logging, GDPR/CCPA/HIPAA/SOX/PCI-DSS compliance.
