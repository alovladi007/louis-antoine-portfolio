Conformal Prediction Mathematical Model

Conformal prediction provides distribution-free prediction intervals:

Conformity Score:

$$s_i = |y_i - \hat{y}_i|$$

Quantile Calculation:

$$\hat{q} = \text{Quantile}\left(\{s_1, s_2, ..., s_n\}, \lceil (n+1)(1-\alpha) \rceil / n\right)$$

Prediction Interval:

$$C_{n+1} = [\hat{y}_{n+1} - \hat{q}, \hat{y}_{n+1} + \hat{q}]$$

Where yᵢ is the true value, ŷᵢ is the predicted value, α is the significance level, and the prediction interval C_{n+1} has coverage probability at least 1-α.
