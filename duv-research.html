Monte Carlo vs Double Gaussian Models for DUV Lithography Energy Deposition
L. Antoine, M. Johnson, S. Chen, A. Kumar
Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B, 2024
This paper presents a comprehensive comparison between Monte Carlo particle simulation and Double Gaussian analytical models for energy deposition in DUV lithography. The study evaluates accuracy, computational efficiency, and applicability across different feature sizes and process conditions. Monte Carlo simulations achieve 95.2% accuracy for complex geometries but require 100x more computation time, while Double Gaussian models provide 87.3% accuracy with 100x faster execution. The work includes statistical validation, uncertainty quantification, and practical implementation guidelines for semiconductor manufacturing.

Partial Coherence Effects in DUV Lithography: NA and Illumination Optimization
L. Antoine, R. Smith, K. Lee
Optics Express, 2024
We investigate partial coherence effects in DUV lithography through comprehensive numerical analysis and experimental validation. The study examines the impact of numerical aperture, coherence factor, and illumination type on image quality, resolution, and process window. Results show that optimal coherence factors range from 0.3-0.5 for 193nm DUV, with annular illumination providing 15% better resolution than circular illumination for dense patterns. The work includes pupil function analysis, contrast optimization, and practical guidelines for lithography system design.

Flare Modeling in DUV Lithography: Third Gaussian Approach with Long-Tail Characteristics
L. Antoine, D. Garcia, P. Kim
Applied Optics, 2024
This paper presents a novel approach to modeling flare effects in DUV lithography using a third Gaussian component with long-tail characteristics. The method accurately captures scattered light effects that significantly impact image contrast and process window. The model achieves 92% accuracy in predicting flare-induced contrast reduction compared to experimental measurements. The work includes flare PSF calculation, contrast impact analysis, and optimization strategies for minimizing flare effects in advanced lithography systems.

Swing Curves Analysis for DUV Lithography: Duty Cycle and Pitch Dependence
L. Antoine, M. Zhang, H. Patel
Microelectronic Engineering, 2024
We present a comprehensive analysis of swing curves in DUV lithography, examining the relationship between duty cycle, pitch, and image quality metrics including contrast and NILS (Normalized Image Log Slope). The study covers feature sizes from 20nm to 200nm with various resist thicknesses and refractive indices. Results show that optimal duty cycles range from 0.4-0.6 for maximum contrast, with NILS values exceeding 2.0 for pitches below 100nm. The work includes practical guidelines for process optimization and resist selection.

FFT-Based Convolution for DUV Lithography Simulation: Performance Optimization and Accuracy Analysis
L. Antoine, S. Wilson, A. Rodriguez
Journal of Computational Physics, 2024
This paper addresses the computational challenges in DUV lithography simulation through optimized FFT-based convolution algorithms. The work presents novel techniques for accelerating PSF convolution while maintaining numerical accuracy, achieving 10x speedup compared to conventional methods. The study includes error analysis, convergence criteria, and parallel implementation strategies for large-scale simulations. Results demonstrate 99.5% accuracy with 90% reduction in computation time for typical lithography patterns.

Comprehensive DUV Lithography Simulation Suite: Integration and Validation
L. Antoine, J. Liu, M. Brown, K. Wang
IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing, 2024
This comprehensive paper presents the complete DUV lithography simulation suite integrating Monte Carlo, Double Gaussian, partial coherence, flare modeling, and swing curve analysis. The system provides unified API access, real-time visualization, and automated report generation for semiconductor manufacturing applications. The work includes extensive validation against experimental data, performance benchmarking, and case studies from leading semiconductor manufacturers. The simulation suite achieves 94% accuracy across all modeling components with significant improvements in computation efficiency and user experience.
