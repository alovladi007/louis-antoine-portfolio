Theoretical Background

Fundamental Physics

Core equations and principles:

Primary equation: R = f(P, T, Γ, E) where R is etch rate, P pressure, T temperature, Γ flux, E energy Transport: ∂n/∂t + ���·Γ = S - L Momentum: m·dv/dt = F_electric + F_collision Energy: dE/dt = P_absorbed - P_radiated - P_collision

Reaction Mechanisms

Surface and gas-phase chemistry:

Adsorption: A(g) + S → A·S (rate: k_ads·P·σ) Reaction: A·S + B·S → Products (rate: k_rxn·θ_A·θ_B·exp(-E_a/kT)) Desorption: Products·S → Products(g) + S (rate: k_des·θ_prod) Overall: Rate-limiting step determines observed kinetics

Transport Phenomena

Mass and energy transfer:

Diffusion: J = -D·∇n (Fick's law) Knudsen regime (high AR): D_K = (d/3)·√(8kT/πm) Continuum regime: D = λ·v_thermal/3 Transition: Kn = λ_mfp / L_characteristic Kn < 0.01: Continuum, Kn > 10: Molecular, 0.01 < Kn < 10: Transition

Statistical Process Control

Uniformity and reproducibility metrics:

Mean: μ = (1/N)·Σx_i Standard deviation: σ = √[(1/N)·Σ(x_i - μ)²] Uniformity: U = (σ/μ)·100% Process capability: C_p = (USL - LSL)/(6σ) C_pk = min[(USL - μ)/(3σ), (μ - LSL)/(3σ)] Target: C_pk > 1.33 for manufacturing

Optimization Strategies

Multi-variable process tuning:

Response surface: y = β_0 + Σβ_i·x_i + Σβ_ij·x_i·x_j + ε Gradient descent: x_(n+1) = x_n - α·∇f(x_n) Simulated annealing: Accept worse solution with P = exp(-ΔE/T) Genetic algorithms: Selection + Crossover + Mutation Constraints: g_i(x) ≤ 0, h_j(x) = 0

Process compatibility and materials issues:

Practical Implementation

Recipe Development Workflow

Systematic approach to process optimization:

1. Define requirements: Rate, CD, profile, selectivity, uniformity 2. Screen chemistries: CF₄, SF₆, Cl₂, HBr based on material 3. DOE exploration: 3-5 factors, 2-3 levels, response surface 4. Optimize: Gradient search near optimum from DOE 5. Verify: Capability study (C_pk), SEM cross-sections 6. Qualify: 30+ wafer run, drift study, matching across tools 7. Production: SPC monitoring, feedback control, PM scheduling

Troubleshooting Guide

Common issues and solutions:

Problem: Non-uniformity Causes: EM field, gas flow, temperature gradient Solutions: Adjust power/freq/gap, optimize flow, heater zones Problem: Poor selectivity Causes: Too much ion bombardment, wrong chemistry Solutions: Lower bias, add polymer-forming gas (CHF₃, C₄F₈) Problem: Micro-trenching Causes: Ion reflection from sidewalls Solutions: Lower pressure, reduce ion energy, adjust angle Problem: Notching Causes: Charging damage Solutions: Pulse plasma, use conducting layers, lower AR

Safety and Best Practices

Critical operational guidelines:

Gas safety: - Always purge with N₂/Ar before venting - Interlock pressure sensors with gas shut-off - Secondary containment for toxic/pyrophoric gases - Regular leak checking (He mass spec) Electrical safety: - RF isolation, grounding verification - Arc detection with immediate shutdown - Regular PM of matching networks Personnel: - Training on MSDS for all process gases - Emergency procedures (gas leak, fire, injury) - Proper PPE: safety glasses, cleanroom garments

Stochastic particle tracking in plasma reactor:

Trajectory integration: dx/dt = v_x, dy/dt = v_y, dz/dt = v_z dv/dt = (q/m)(E + v×B) + F_collision Collision probability: P_coll(Δt) = 1 - exp(-ν_total·Δt) where ν_total = Σ_i n_i σ_i v_rel Collision partner selection: P_i = ν_i / ν_total Scattering angle: Elastic: cos(θ_cm) = 1 + 2ξ_1[(1-ξ_2)(1+α)-1]/(1+α-ξ_2(1-α)) Inelastic: Use measured differential cross sections

Industrial Case Studies

Case Study 1: 7nm Logic Contact Etch

Ultra-high aspect ratio via etching:

Requirements: - CD: 30nm ± 2nm (3σ) - Depth: 800nm (AR > 26:1) - Profile: 90° ± 1° - SiO₂:Si₃N₄ selectivity > 20:1 - LWR < 3nm Solution approach: 1. Hard mask stack: SiO₂/Si₃N₄/SiO₂ 2. Pulsed plasma (10ms on / 50ms off) - reduce charging 3. CF₄/CHF₃/Ar chemistry - tune polymer 4. Cryogenic cooling (-20°C) - minimize roughness 5. Multi-step: Breakthrough → Main → Over-etch Results: - CD uniformity: 1.8% (3σ) - Depth loading: < 3% - Yield improvement: 12% vs. conventional - Cost: -15% via higher throughput

Case Study 2: 3D NAND Channel Hole

Etching through 128 oxide/nitride pairs:

Challenge: 256 layers × 2 materials, AR > 60:1, < 100nm diameter Process innovation: - Cyclic etch/deposition to control profile - Etch: CF₄/O₂/Ar, 15s, breaks through SiO₂ - Deposit: C₄F₈, 3s, sidewall passivation - 150+ cycles for full depth (> 12μm) Critical parameters: - Pressure gradient: 10→5 mTorr (top to bottom) - Power modulation: Sync with etch/dep cycles - Temperature: Strict ±2°C control Achieved: - Depth uniformity: 2.5% across wafer - Bowing: < 5nm over 12μm - Profile angle: 89.8° ± 0.3° - Throughput: 15 wafers/hour

Case Study 3: GaN Power Device Mesa Etch

III-V semiconductor etching challenges:

Material: GaN/AlGaN heterostructure Requirements: - Smooth surface: Ra < 5Å - Minimal subsurface damage - Etch stop at AlGaN layer Chemistry: Cl₂/BCl₃/Ar - Cl₂: Primary etchant for Ga and N - BCl₃: Sidewall passivation - Ar: Physical component for anisotropy Optimized conditions: - Pressure: 5 mTorr (minimize roughness) - ICP: 500W, Bias: -120V (control damage) - Temperature: 250°C (volatile products) - Endpoint: Laser interferometry Results vs. conventional: - Roughness: 3Å vs. 15Å - Leakage current: 10× lower - Breakdown voltage: +20% - Device yield: 85% → 94%

Emerging Technologies

Cryogenic Etching

Ultra-low temperature processing:

Operating regime: T < -100°C (He cooling) Advantages: - Suppressed chemical reactions → anisotropy - Reduced diffusion → sharp profiles - No polymer formation → clean process - Enhanced physisorption Applications: - Silicon: Perfectly vertical, smooth walls - Organics: Direct pattern transfer without hardmask - Metals: Chlorine-based with minimal corrosion Challenges: - Wafer cooling uniformity - Moisture condensation - Extended pump-down times - He cost

Atomic Layer Etching at Scale

Production implementation of ALE:

Throughput challenge: Conventional: 60 wafers/hour @ 300nm/min ALE: 15 wafers/hour @ 3 cycles/min × 0.3nm/cycle Solutions: 1. Spatial ALE: Rotating wafer under segmented head - Modification zone | Purge | Removal zone | Purge - Continuous processing, 4× throughput boost 2. Parallel processing: 4-wafer batch reactor - Shared gas system - Synchronized pulsing 3. Fast cycling: 0.5s per half-cycle - Rapid gas switching valves - Optimized chamber design (low volume) Target: 40 wafers/hour with < 1Å control

Selective Etching by Area

Material and topography selectivity:

Concept: Etch one material while preserving another Mechanisms: 1. Inherent chemical: Si vs. SiO₂ in HF vapor 2. Plasma-induced: Si vs. SiGe in CF₄/O₂ 3. Inhibitor-enhanced: Block Si with NH₃, etch SiGe Applications: - Gate-all-around FET: Remove Si, leave SiGe fins - Self-aligned contacts: Stop on metal, continue on oxide - Backside power delivery: Through-oxide vias Requirements: - Selectivity > 100:1 (ideally infinite) - Low damage on non-etched material - Compatible with subsequent processing
