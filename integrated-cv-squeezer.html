Theoretical Background

Squeezed light generation via optical parametric processes enables quantum-enhanced sensing below the shot-noise limit. Integrated photonics platforms (SiN, TFLN) offer compact, low-loss implementations of χ⁽²⁾ and χ⁽³⁾ nonlinearities.

Kerr (χ⁽³⁾) Four-Wave Mixing Squeezing

Sout(Ω) = 1 - ηesc · (4Gκ²) / [(κ + Γ)² + Ω²]

Vdet = ηtotal · Vonchip + (1 - ηtotal) · Vvac

SdB = 10 log₁₀(Vdet / Vshot)

Where: G = parametric gain (pump-dependent), κ = total cavity decay rate, Γ = internal loss rate, ηesc = escape efficiency, ηtotal = collection × coupling × detection efficiency

χ⁽²⁾ OPA (Optical Parametric Amplification)

Vx = e-2r, Vp = e+2r

r = (χ(2) · Epump · L) / (2 · √(ε₀ · c · ħω))

Squeezing parameter r increases with nonlinearity χ⁽²⁾, pump field Epump, and interaction length L. TFLN offers strong χ⁽²⁾ (~30 pm/V) for compact squeezers.
