Modeling Energy Deposition in DUV Masks: Monte Carlo vs. Double Gaussian

Advanced Lithography Simulation & Analysis

Project Overview

Complete simulator comparing aerial image energy deposition predicted by Double-Gaussian PSF (FFT convolution) against Monte Carlo particle model. Features partial coherence knobs, flare modeling, swing curves, and comprehensive analysis with polished PDF report generation.

Key Features

  • Monte Carlo particle simulation for energy deposition modeling
  • Double Gaussian PSF with FFT convolution
  • Partial coherence modeling with NA/illumination effects
  • Flare modeling with third Gaussian (long-tail effects)
  • Swing curves vs duty cycle and pitch analysis
  • Comprehensive statistical analysis and validation
  • PDF report generation with figures and equations

Technical Specifications

  • 193nm DUV wavelength simulation
  • Numerical aperture optimization
  • Statistical uncertainty quantification
  • Advanced lithography simulation algorithms
  • Performance metrics and validation

Technology Stack

Python Monte Carlo FFT Convolution Partial Coherence DUV Lithography Statistical Analysis
