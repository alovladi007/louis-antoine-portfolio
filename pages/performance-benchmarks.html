Benchmarking Methodology

Testing Environment

  • Hardware: NVIDIA RTX 3080, Intel i7-12700K, 32GB DDR4 RAM
  • Software: Ubuntu 20.04, CUDA 11.8, PyTorch 1.13, OpenCV 4.6
  • Testing Protocol: 1000 inference runs per model, warm-up runs excluded
  • Metrics: Mean inference time, standard deviation, 95th percentile

Evaluation Criteria

  • Accuracy: mAP@0.5 and mAP@0.5:0.95 on validation datasets
  • Speed: Average inference time and FPS measurements
  • Efficiency: Power consumption and memory usage
  • Robustness: Performance across different weather and lighting conditions
Note: All benchmarks are conducted on standardized test images (640x640 resolution) to ensure fair comparison across different models and hardware configurations.
