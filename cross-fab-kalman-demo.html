Kalman Filter Mathematical Model

The Kalman filter algorithm consists of prediction and update steps:

Prediction Step:

$$\hat{x}_{k|k-1} = F\hat{x}_{k-1|k-1} + Bu_k$$

$$P_{k|k-1} = FP_{k-1|k-1}F^T + Q$$

Update Step:

$$K_k = P_{k|k-1}H^T(HP_{k|k-1}H^T + R)^{-1}$$

$$\hat{x}_{k|k} = \hat{x}_{k|k-1} + K_k(z_k - H\hat{x}_{k|k-1})$$

$$P_{k|k} = (I - K_kH)P_{k|k-1}$$

Where F is the state transition matrix, H is the observation matrix, Q is process noise covariance, R is measurement noise covariance, and K is the Kalman gain.
