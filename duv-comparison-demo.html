Monte Carlo Model
Particle-based simulation with statistical sampling
Pros:
• High accuracy for complex geometries
• Handles arbitrary shapes
• Statistical uncertainty quantification
• Physically realistic
Cons:
• Computationally expensive
• Requires many particles
• Statistical noise
• Longer simulation time
Double Gaussian Model
Analytical PSF with FFT convolution
Pros:
• Very fast computation
• Deterministic results
• Low memory usage
• Easy to implement
Cons:
• Limited to simple geometries
• Approximate solution
• Less physically accurate
• Fixed PSF shape
