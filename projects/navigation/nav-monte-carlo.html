Monte Carlo Analysis

Monte Carlo simulation is essential for understanding real-world navigation performance where sensor characteristics vary, environmental conditions change, and random errors accumulate differently in each mission. By running thousands of simulations with randomized parameters, we can:

  • Characterize mean performance and variance
  • Identify worst-case scenarios and failure modes
  • Compute reliability metrics and success probabilities
  • Validate filter tuning and sensor redundancy strategies
  • Predict performance under varying environmental conditions
