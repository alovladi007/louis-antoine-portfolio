Groundwater Isolation

GRACE TWS = Groundwater + Soil Moisture + Surface Water + Snow. GW is isolated by subtracting model estimates of other components (GLDAS, CLM).

Limitations

~300 km spatial resolution limits detection of local depletion. Separation uncertainty from hydrological models can reach 20-30% of signal.

GRACE Ice Mass

Gravity changes over ice sheets directly measure mass changes. Must correct for post-glacial rebound (GIA) which causes apparent mass gain.

Sea Level Rise

1 Gt of ice = 0.0028 mm global sea level rise. Greenland and Antarctica together contribute ~1 mm/yr to global sea level.

GRACE-DSI

Drought Severity Index derived from GRACE TWS anomalies normalized by historical variability. Provides integrated measure of total water deficit.

Advantages over SPI/PDSI

GRACE captures total column water storage including groundwater, providing earlier drought detection and more complete assessment than precipitation-only indices.

Operational Use

GRACE data assimilated into land surface models (NASA LIS) for operational drought monitoring by USDA and international agencies.
