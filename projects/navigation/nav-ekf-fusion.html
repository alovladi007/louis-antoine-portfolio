About This Demo

The Extended Kalman Filter is the heart of the navigation system, fusing all sensor measurements into an optimal state estimate. Toggle sensors on/off to see how the filter adapts. Watch covariance evolution, Kalman gain dynamics, and innovation sequences in real-time. The 15-state error vector includes position, velocity, attitude errors plus gyro and accelerometer biases.
