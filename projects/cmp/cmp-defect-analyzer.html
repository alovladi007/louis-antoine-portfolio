CMP Defect Analyzer

Comprehensive defect classification and root cause analysis tool for Chemical Mechanical Planarization processes. Identify, classify, and diagnose scratches, particles, corrosion, dishing, and erosion defects with automated corrective action recommendations.

Defect Types & Analysis

This tool analyzes five major CMP defect categories: Scratches (mechanical damage), Particles (contamination), Corrosion (chemical attack), Dishing (over-polishing in features), and Erosion (pattern-dependent removal). The system provides root cause analysis and corrective actions based on industry best practices.
