About Tribochemical CMP

Metal CMP combines mechanical wear and chemical corrosion in a synergistic process. The mechanical action removes the oxidized layer while chemical oxidizers continuously form new oxide. This tribochemical mechanism is essential for copper and tungsten CMP.

Tribochemical Theory

Kaufman Mechanism

The Kaufman model describes metal CMP as a wear-corrosion process where mechanical action removes the chemically formed oxide layer:

MRRtotal = MRRmech + MRRchem + MRRsynergy

Oxidation Kinetics

The formation of metal oxide follows Arrhenius kinetics:

kox = A × exp(-Ea / RT) × [Oxidizer]n

pH Effects

pH strongly influences the dissolution rate and passivation behavior. Copper is stable in acidic pH (4-8), while tungsten prefers acidic conditions (2-4).

Passivation Layer

The steady-state oxide thickness hss balances formation and removal rates. Thinner oxides lead to higher MRR but risk substrate damage.

Material-Specific Considerations

  • Copper CMP: Uses H₂O₂ as oxidizer in slightly acidic pH (4-6). Forms Cu₂O and CuO layers.
  • Tungsten CMP: Requires strong oxidizers (Fe³⁺, IO₃⁻) at low pH (2-4). Forms WO₃ passive layer.
  • Aluminum CMP: Uses mild oxidizers at moderate pH (4-5). Forms Al₂O₃ protective layer.

Process Optimization

Optimal tribochemical CMP requires balancing several factors:

  • Mechanical wear must be sufficient to remove oxide but not damage substrate
  • Chemical oxidation rate should match mechanical removal rate
  • pH must be controlled to prevent excessive corrosion or passivation
  • Temperature affects both chemical kinetics and mechanical properties
  • Slurry flow ensures fresh oxidizer delivery and byproduct removal

Advanced Models

Modern tribochemical models incorporate:

  • Electrochemical potential measurements for real-time monitoring
  • Surface roughness evolution during polishing
  • Multi-layer oxide structures (e.g., Cu/Cu₂O/CuO)
  • Complexing agents that modify dissolution rates
  • Inhibitor effects on passivation stability

Practical Applications

Understanding tribochemical mechanisms enables:

  • Recipe development for new metal materials
  • Optimization of process windows for high selectivity
  • Defect reduction through controlled passivation
  • Extended pad life by matching chemical and mechanical rates
  • Cost reduction through lower oxidizer consumption
