Project Overview

Hardware Design

  • Synchronous buck topology with GaN FETs for reduced switching losses
  • Multi-phase interleaving (4-phase) for ripple cancellation
  • Integrated gate drivers with adaptive dead-time control
  • 4-layer PCB with optimized thermal vias and copper pours

Digital Control

  • STM32G4 MCU with 12-bit ADC at 5 MSPS
  • Digital PID with anti-windup and bumpless transfer
  • FPGA-based PWM generation for 10ns resolution
  • Real-time efficiency monitoring and MPPT algorithm
