Shot Noise Units (SNU)

In CV-QKD, noise is measured relative to the shot noise unit (SNU), which represents the quantum vacuum noise variance.

• Shot noise = 1 SNU (by definition)

• Excess noise ξ typically ranges from 0.001 to 0.05 SNU

• Electronic noise should be < 0.01 SNU for good performance

• Channel-added noise χ_line = (1-T)/T grows exponentially with distance
