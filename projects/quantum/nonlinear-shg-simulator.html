SHG Theory

Second Harmonic Generation

Two photons at frequency ω combine to create one photon at 2ω through the χ⁽²⁾ nonlinearity:

P2ω = ε₀ χ⁽²⁾ Eω2

Phase Matching Condition

Efficient conversion requires momentum conservation:

Δk = k2ω - 2kω = 0

Quasi-Phase Matching

Periodic modulation of χ⁽²⁾ compensates phase mismatch:

ΛQPM = 2π / Δk
