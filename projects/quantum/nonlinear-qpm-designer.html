QPM Theory

Quasi-Phase Matching

Periodic modulation of χ⁽²⁾ provides momentum to compensate phase mismatch:

k2ω - 2kω - Kg = 0

where Kg = 2π/Λ is the grating vector

Effective Nonlinearity

The effective χ⁽²⁾ for first-order QPM:

χ(2)eff = (2/π) χ(2) sin(πD)

Maximum at 50% duty cycle (D = 0.5)
