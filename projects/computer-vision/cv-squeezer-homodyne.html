Homodyne Detection Physics

Balanced homodyne detection measures quadrature operators X̂_θ = X̂cosθ + P̂sinθ by interfering signal with strong local oscillator (LO). Common-mode rejection cancels classical noise, achieving shot-noise-limited sensitivity.

Quadrature Operator:
X̂_θ = (â e^(-iθ) + â† e^(iθ)) / √2
θ = 0: Position X̂, θ = π/2: Momentum P̂
[X̂, P̂] = i (canonical commutation)
Balanced Homodyne Photocurrent:
i_HD = η · e · √(P_LO · P_sig) · cos(φ) · X_θ
P_LO >> P_sig (strong LO approximation)
φ = relative phase between signal & LO
Measured Variance (AC Component):
⟨Δi²⟩ = (η · e · P_LO)² · ⟨ΔX_θ²⟩
Shot noise: ⟨ΔX_θ²⟩_SN = 1 (vacuum)
Squeezing: ⟨ΔX_θ²⟩_sq = e^(-2r) (θ aligned)
Common-Mode Rejection Ratio:
CMRR = 20 · log₁₀(A_CM / A_diff)
Ideal balanced detector: CMRR > 60 dB
Cancels laser intensity noise, electronics noise
