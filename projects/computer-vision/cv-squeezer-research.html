Theory & Background

Kerr χ⁽³⁾ FWM Theory

Four-wave mixing in SiN rings, degenerate parametric amplification, dispersion engineering, group velocity dispersion compensation, and cavity-enhanced squeezing spectrum

χ⁽²⁾ OPA Theory

Optical parametric amplification in TFLN waveguides, quasi-phase-matching, spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC), type-0/I/II phase matching, and pump depletion

Platform Design & Optimization

SiN Ring Resonators

Microring design with Q-factor optimization, coupling gap engineering, thermal tuning, FSR calculation, mode family analysis, and fabrication tolerance simulation

TFLN Waveguides

Thin-film lithium niobate waveguide design, periodic poling for QPM, electro-optic tuning, mode confinement, nonlinear coefficient optimization, and ridge/rib geometries

Simulation & Analysis Tools

Squeezing Spectrum Analyzer

Frequency-dependent squeezing spectrum calculation using input-output Langevin formalism, cavity linewidth effects, sideband resolution, and phase-sensitive detection

Parametric Gain vs Pump Power

Pump power sweep simulation showing parametric gain threshold, saturation behavior, quantum conversion efficiency, and thermal effects at high power

Loss Budget & Efficiency

Complete loss waterfall from on-chip to detected squeezing: propagation loss, coupling loss, escape efficiency, collection efficiency, detector QE, and noise budget

Gaussian State Visualizer

Wigner function visualization, covariance matrix representation, symplectic eigenvalues, purity calculation, and entanglement criteria for two-mode squeezed states

Detection & Characterization

Balanced Homodyne Detection

Homodyne detector simulation with LO phase scan, quadrature measurement, shot-noise calibration, electronic noise modeling, common-mode rejection, and visibility optimization

Quantum Noise Analysis

Quantum vs classical noise comparison, vacuum fluctuations, thermal noise, amplifier noise, phase noise from pump laser, and Heisenberg uncertainty limit

Design Guidelines

Practical design rules for squeezer implementation: material selection, geometry optimization, fabrication constraints, packaging requirements, and performance-cost tradeoffs
