Loss Budget Physics

Total system efficiency is the product of individual loss channels: propagation loss, coupling loss, escape efficiency, fiber-chip coupling, and detector quantum efficiency. Each dB of loss adds vacuum noise, degrading squeezing.

Total Transmission Efficiency:
η_total = η_prop · η_coupling · η_escape · η_fiber · η_det
All losses multiply to give end-to-end transmission
Measured Squeezing with Loss:
S_measured = η_total · S_ideal + (1 - η_total)
Loss mixes vacuum noise: each 1% loss adds 1% vacuum
S_measured(dB) = 10 · log₁₀(S_measured)
Propagation Loss:
η_prop = exp(-α · L)
α = α_material + α_scattering + α_absorption
Typical SiN: 0.1-1 dB/cm
Escape Efficiency:
η_escape = κ_ext / (κ_ext + κ_int)
Critical coupling: κ_ext = κ_int → η_escape = 50%
Overcoupled: κ_ext > κ_int → η_escape > 50%
Noise Budget (Variance Units):
V_total = η_total · V_squeezed + (1 - η_total) · V_vacuum
V_vacuum = 1 (shot noise reference)
V_squeezed = exp(-2r) for squeezing parameter r
