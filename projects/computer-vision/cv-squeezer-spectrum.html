Cavity Squeezing Spectrum Physics

The squeezing spectrum of a cavity-enhanced parametric process exhibits Lorentzian rolloff at high frequencies due to cavity response time. Peak squeezing occurs at DC (Ω = 0), with 3dB bandwidth determined by total cavity linewidth κ_total.

Cavity-Enhanced Squeezing Spectrum (Kerr FWM):
S(Ω) = 1 - η_esc · [4G · κ²] / [(κ_total + κ_int)² + Ω²]
G = parametric gain (dimensionless)
Ω = 2πf = analysis frequency offset from carrier
OPA Squeezing Spectrum (χ⁽²⁾):
S(Ω) = cosh(2r) - sinh(2r) · [κ_ext² / (κ_total² + Ω²)]
r = squeezing parameter
κ_ext = external coupling rate
3dB Squeezing Bandwidth:
Δf_3dB = κ_total / (2π)
κ_total = κ_ext + κ_int (external + intrinsic loss)
Anti-Squeezing Quadrature:
S_anti(Ω) = 1 + η_esc · [4G · κ²] / [(κ_total + κ_int)² + Ω²]
(Noise amplification in orthogonal quadrature)
Shot Noise Reference:
S_SN = 1 (vacuum fluctuations)
S_dB = 10 · log₁₀(S(Ω))
