Pump-Dependent Parametric Gain Physics

Parametric gain scales linearly with pump power at low powers but saturates due to: pump depletion, thermal bistability, and higher-order nonlinearities. Optimal pump power balances high squeezing against thermal drift and gain saturation.

Unsaturated Parametric Gain (Kerr):
G = (γ · P_pump · L_eff) / (ħω · FSR)
γ = (2π · n₂) / (λ · A_eff) = Kerr coefficient
L_eff = effective interaction length
Saturated Gain (Pump Depletion):
G_sat = G₀ / √(1 + P_pump / P_sat)
P_sat = saturation power (material-dependent)
Thermal Resonance Shift:
Δλ_thermal = (dn/dT) · (α_abs · P_pump · L) / (κ_thermal · A_mode) · λ
α_abs = absorption coefficient (dB/cm)
κ_thermal = thermal conductivity
Conversion Efficiency (OPA):
η_conversion = tanh²(r) = tanh²(√G · L)
Max efficiency: 100% at r → ∞
Two-Photon Absorption (TPA) Loss:
α_TPA = β_TPA · I_pump
β_TPA = TPA coefficient (cm/GW for Si)
Reduces effective gain at high pump intensity
