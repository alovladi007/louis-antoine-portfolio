TFLN Waveguide Physics

Thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) on insulator enables χ⁽²⁾ optical parametric amplification with: electro-optic tuning (Pockels effect), periodic poling for quasi-phase-matching, and tight mode confinement for enhanced nonlinear interaction.

Effective Nonlinear Coefficient (QPM):
d_eff = (2/π) · d₃₃ · |sin(πm/2)|
d₃₃ = 27 pm/V for LN
m = poling order (1, 3, 5, ...)
Quasi-Phase-Matching Period:
Λ = λ_p / (2 · Δn)
Δn = n_p - (n_s + n_i)/2
Typical: Λ ≈ 3-10 μm for degenerate SPDC
Electro-Optic Wavelength Shift:
Δλ/ΔV = -(n³ · r₃₃ · λ) / (2 · d · n_eff)
r₃₃ = 30.8 pm/V (Pockels coefficient)
d = electrode gap
Waveguide Mode Confinement:
Γ = ∫∫_core |E|² dA / ∫∫_all |E|² dA
A_eff = (∫∫ |E|² dA)² / ∫∫ |E|⁴ dA
Parametric Gain Coefficient:
g = (2 · ω_s · d_eff² · P_pump) / (ε₀ · c³ · n_p · n_s · n_i · A_eff)
Squeezing: r = g · L
