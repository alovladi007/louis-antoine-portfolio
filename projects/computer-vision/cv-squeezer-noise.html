Noise Sources in Quantum Measurements

Optical measurements are limited by quantum shot noise (vacuum fluctuations), classical technical noise (laser RIN, thermal), and detector noise. Squeezing breaks the Standard Quantum Limit (SQL) by reducing vacuum noise below shot noise level.

Shot Noise (Poisson Statistics):
⟨Δn²⟩_shot = ⟨n⟩
SNR_shot = √⟨n⟩ (photon number)
Standard Quantum Limit: SNR_SQL = √(ħω/2kT)
Relative Intensity Noise (RIN):
RIN(f) = S_I(f) / ⟨I⟩² [dBc/Hz]
Typical laser: RIN ≈ -140 to -160 dBc/Hz
Suppressed by CMRR in balanced detection
Thermal (Johnson-Nyquist) Noise:
⟨V_th²⟩ = 4 · k_B · T · R · Δf
T = temperature, R = resistance, Δf = bandwidth
Dominant at low frequencies (< 1 MHz)
Quantum Noise Reduction:
S_squeezed(f) = e^(-2r) · S_shot(f)
S_total = S_squeezed + S_classical + S_thermal
Squeezing advantage: S_total < S_shot when S_classical << S_shot
Noise Figure (NF):
NF = SNR_in / SNR_out [dB]
Ideal amplifier: NF = 3 dB (quantum limit)
Phase-sensitive amp: NF = 0 dB (noiseless for one quad)
