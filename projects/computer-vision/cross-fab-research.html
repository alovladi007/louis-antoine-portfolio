Published Research

Virtual Metrology for Advanced Process Control in Semiconductor Manufacturing
L. Antoine (Work in Progress)
Target: IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing
This work presents a comprehensive virtual metrology framework using CatBoost machine learning algorithms for predicting critical dimensions (CD) and overlay in semiconductor manufacturing. The proposed approach aims to achieve high accuracy in CD and overlay prediction while significantly reducing metrology sampling requirements. The framework includes conformal prediction for uncertainty quantification and real-time adaptation capabilities for production environments.

Reference: Virtual Metrology in Semiconductor Manufacturing
Khan, A., Moyne, J., & Tilbury, D.
IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing, 2007
This foundational paper introduces virtual metrology concepts in semiconductor manufacturing, establishing the theoretical framework for predicting process outputs using historical data and process parameters. The work presents early machine learning approaches for CD prediction and discusses the integration of virtual metrology with run-to-run control systems.

Reference: Run-to-Run Control in Semiconductor Manufacturing
Butler, S. W., & Stefani, J. A.
IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing, 1994
This seminal paper presents the double exponentially weighted moving average (Double-EWMA) control algorithm for semiconductor manufacturing. The work establishes the mathematical foundation for run-to-run control and demonstrates its effectiveness in reducing process variation and improving yield in production environments.
PDF IEEE Xplore Cite
Reference: Statistical Process Control for Semiconductor Manufacturing
Montgomery, D. C., & Mastrangelo, C. M.
Journal of Quality Technology, 1991
This comprehensive work presents statistical process control methods specifically adapted for semiconductor manufacturing. The paper covers multivariate control charts, Hotelling's T² statistics, and fault detection algorithms that form the foundation of modern SPC systems in semiconductor fabs.
PDF Taylor & Francis Cite
Reference: Multi-Objective Optimization in Process Control
Deb, K., Pratap, A., Agarwal, S., & Meyarivan, T.
IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation, 2002
This influential paper presents the NSGA-II algorithm for multi-objective optimization, which has been widely adopted in process control applications. The work establishes the theoretical foundation for Pareto-optimal solutions and provides practical guidelines for implementing multi-objective optimization in manufacturing systems.
PDF IEEE Xplore Cite
Reference: Conformal Prediction for Machine Learning
Vovk, V., Gammerman, A., & Shafer, G.
Journal of Machine Learning Research, 2005
This foundational paper introduces conformal prediction methods for providing reliable prediction intervals in machine learning applications. The work presents the theoretical framework for uncertainty quantification and demonstrates its effectiveness in providing statistically valid confidence intervals for predictions.
