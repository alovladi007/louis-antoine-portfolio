Ethical Trading Guidelines

Principles for responsible algorithmic trading and market integrity

Core Ethical Principles

Market Integrity

Never engage in market manipulation, spoofing, or layering. Respect fair and orderly markets.

Transparency

Maintain clear documentation of trading strategies and their potential market impact.

Fairness

Avoid strategies that exploit information asymmetry at the expense of retail investors.

Data Privacy

Handle all market data and client information with appropriate security measures.

Prohibited Practices

  • Market Manipulation: No artificial price inflation or deflation
  • Front-Running: No trading ahead of known client orders
  • Spoofing: No placing orders with intent to cancel before execution
  • Wash Trading: No trades between related accounts to create false volume
  • Insider Trading: No trading on material non-public information

Regulatory Compliance

All algorithmic trading must comply with SEC, FINRA, MiFID II, and other applicable regulations. Consult with compliance and legal teams before deployment.

Social Responsibility

Consider the broader impact of trading algorithms on market stability, price discovery, and systemic risk. Implement circuit breakers and risk controls to prevent contribution to flash crashes or market disruptions.
