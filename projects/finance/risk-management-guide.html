Risk Management Framework

Comprehensive guide to position sizing, stop-loss strategies, and portfolio risk controls

Core Principles

Position Sizing

Never risk more than 1-2% of portfolio on a single trade. Use Kelly Criterion for optimal sizing.

Stop Losses

Always use stop-losses. ATR-based stops adapt to volatility conditions.

Diversification

Diversify across assets, strategies, and timeframes to reduce correlation risk.

Drawdown Limits

Set maximum drawdown thresholds that trigger automatic position reduction.

Position Sizing Methods

Kelly Criterion

Optimal position size that maximizes long-term growth rate:

f* = (p * b - q) / b

Where:
f* = Fraction of capital to risk
p = Probability of winning
q = Probability of losing (1 - p)
b = Win/Loss ratio

Volatility-Based Sizing

Scale position size inversely with volatility:

Position Size = (Risk Budget) / (ATR × Multiplier)

Risk Controls

  • Daily Loss Limit: Stop trading if daily loss exceeds 3% of portfolio
  • Maximum Position Size: No single position exceeds 10% of portfolio
  • Correlation Limits: Monitor and limit exposure to correlated assets
  • Leverage Constraints: Maximum 2x leverage across portfolio
  • Liquidity Requirements: Only trade assets with sufficient volume
