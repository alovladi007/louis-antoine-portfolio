LPI/LPD Design Principles

Power Control

Transmit at minimum power needed for reliable reception. Use adaptive power control based on range.

Bandwidth Spreading

Spread signal across wide bandwidth to reduce PSD below noise floor. Higher SF = lower detectability.

Randomization

Use crypto-secure PN codes. Avoid periodic patterns that can be detected by cyclostationary analysis.

Emission Control

Minimize transmission duration. Use burst mode with store-and-forward when possible.
