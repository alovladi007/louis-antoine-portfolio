3D RF/High‑Speed System‑in‑Package Co‑Design for 5G mmWave and Chiplet Interconnects
L. Antoine
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Connecticut
Target: IEEE Transactions on Components, Packaging and Manufacturing Technology
Abstract: We present a unified co‑design methodology for mmWave RF front‑ends and high‑speed chiplet interconnects in advanced System‑in‑Package (SiP) platforms. The approach integrates phased‑array beamforming, package transition S‑parameters, PDN impedance shaping, thermal management, and signal integrity for UCIe links. Results demonstrate sidelobe level (SLL) reduction of 6–10 dB, PDN Zpeak reductions of 8–12 dB, and thermal junction temperature improvements of 10–18 °C under realistic workloads.
1. Introduction
5G mmWave and heterogeneous chiplet systems demand concurrent optimization across EM, thermal, SI/PI, and mechanical domains. Traditional serial flows fail to capture cross‑domain trade‑offs; we propose a parallel co‑design loop with quantitative objectives and automated exploration.

4. Discussion
The co‑optimization shifts package resonance away from data‑rich spectral bands, reduces coupling through spacing and shielding, and balances decap ESR/ESL to flatten Z(f). Thermal benefits derive from optimized heat spreaders and via farms with minimal RF penalty.
5. Conclusion
This work demonstrates a practical co‑design strategy achieving measurable improvements across RF beamforming, PDN stability, and thermal headroom in a unified SiP framework suitable for production design flows.
