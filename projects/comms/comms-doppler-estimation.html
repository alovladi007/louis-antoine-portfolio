Underwater Doppler Effects

Frequency Shift

Doppler shift in underwater acoustics:

fd = fc × v/c

At 25 kHz, 2 m/s motion causes ~33 Hz shift

Time Scaling

Signal duration changes:

α = 1 + v/c

Causes symbol timing errors and ISI without compensation

Compensation

Two-stage process:

  • Resampling for time scaling
  • Frequency correction via NCO
