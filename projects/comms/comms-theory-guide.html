1. Underwater Acoustic Communication

1.1 Acoustic Propagation

Underwater acoustic channels are characterized by severe frequency-dependent attenuation, multipath propagation, and Doppler effects. The Thorp attenuation model describes absorption loss:

$$\alpha(f) = \frac{0.11f^2}{1+f^2} + \frac{44f^2}{4100+f^2} + 2.75 \times 10^{-4}f^2 + 0.003 \text{ dB/km}$$

1.2 OFDM for Underwater Acoustics

Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing combats frequency-selective fading by dividing the channel into parallel narrowband subchannels:

$$s(t) = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} X_k e^{j2\pi f_k t}, \quad f_k = f_0 + k\Delta f$$ where \(N\) is the number of subcarriers and \(\Delta f = 1/T_s\) ensures orthogonality.

1.3 Channel Capacity

Shannon capacity for underwater acoustic channels:

$$C = B \log_2\left(1 + \frac{P}{N_0 B \cdot A(f,d)}\right) \text{ bps}$$ where \(A(f,d) = d^k \cdot 10^{\alpha(f)d/10}\) includes geometric spreading and absorption.

2. Blue-Green Optical Communication

2.1 Optical Absorption Window

Blue-green wavelengths (450-550nm) experience minimum absorption in seawater. Beer-Lambert law describes attenuation:

$$I(d) = I_0 e^{-c(\lambda)d}$$ where \(c(\lambda)\) is wavelength-dependent attenuation coefficient (typically 0.05-0.3 m\(^{-1}\)).

2.2 Link Budget

Received optical power:

$$P_r = P_t \cdot \frac{A_r}{4\pi d^2} \cdot e^{-c(\lambda)d} \cdot \cos(\theta)$$ where \(A_r\) is receiver aperture area, \(d\) is range, and \(\theta\) is misalignment angle.

3. Cross-Domain Networking

3.1 Tri-Link Architecture

The three communication domains operate at vastly different data rates and ranges:

  • Acoustic: 350-900 bps at 0.8-1.2 km (long range, low bandwidth)
  • Optical: 2-8 Mbps at 5-20 m (short range, high bandwidth)
  • RF Relay: High bandwidth surface-to-shore via buoy gateway

3.2 Protocol Stack

Cross-layer optimization is essential due to long propagation delays (acoustic) and intermittent connectivity (optical).

End-to-end latency: \(\tau_{e2e} = \tau_{acoustic} + \tau_{optical} + \tau_{RF} + \tau_{processing}\) Target: \(\tau_{e2e} < 250\) ms
