Underwater Acoustic Channel Considerations

Multipath Fading

Strong interleaving required to combat burst errors from multipath. Typical delay spreads: 10-100ms.

Doppler Spread

Time-varying channel requires adaptive coding rates. Doppler: 1-10 Hz typical underwater.

Limited Bandwidth

Acoustic bandwidth 1-50 kHz limits throughput. Higher code rates preferred when possible.
