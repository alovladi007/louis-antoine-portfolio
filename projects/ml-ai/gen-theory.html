Theory & Background

Mathematical foundations of generative models for scientific data

Variational Autoencoders (VAE)

VAEs learn a probabilistic latent representation by maximizing a lower bound on the data likelihood (ELBO):

$$\mathcal{L}(\theta, \phi; x) = \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi(z|x)}[\log p_\theta(x|z)] - D_{KL}(q_\phi(z|x) \| p(z))$$

The first term is the reconstruction loss, while the KL divergence regularizes the latent space toward a prior \(p(z) = \mathcal{N}(0, I)\).

Reparameterization Trick

To enable backpropagation through stochastic sampling:

$$z = \mu_\phi(x) + \sigma_\phi(x) \odot \epsilon, \quad \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$$

Diffusion Models

Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs) define a forward noising process and learn to reverse it:

Forward Process

$$q(x_t | x_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{1-\beta_t} x_{t-1}, \beta_t I)$$

Reverse Process

$$p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t) = \mathcal{N}(x_{t-1}; \mu_\theta(x_t, t), \Sigma_\theta(x_t, t))$$

Training Objective

The simplified loss predicts the noise:

$$\mathcal{L}_{simple} = \mathbb{E}_{t, x_0, \epsilon}\left[\|\epsilon - \epsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\|^2\right]$$

Normalizing Flows

Flows transform a simple distribution through invertible mappings with tractable Jacobians:

$$\log p_X(x) = \log p_Z(f^{-1}(x)) + \log \left|\det \frac{\partial f^{-1}}{\partial x}\right|$$

For a composition of \(K\) transformations:

$$\log p_X(x) = \log p_Z(z_0) - \sum_{k=1}^{K} \log \left|\det \frac{\partial f_k}{\partial z_{k-1}}\right|$$

E(3) Equivariance

For 3D molecular generation, networks should be equivariant to rotations and translations:

$$f(Rx + t, h) = (Rf_x(x, h) + t, f_h(x, h))$$

Equivariant Graph Neural Networks (EGNNs) achieve this through message passing:

$$m_{ij} = \phi_m(h_i, h_j, \|x_i - x_j\|^2, a_{ij})$$ $$x_i' = x_i + \sum_{j \neq i} (x_i - x_j) \phi_x(m_{ij})$$ $$h_i' = \phi_h\left(h_i, \sum_{j \neq i} m_{ij}\right)$$

Evaluation Metrics

Validity

Fraction of generated samples that are chemically valid molecules.

Uniqueness

Fraction of valid samples that are unique (no duplicates).

Novelty

Fraction of unique samples not present in the training set.

FCD (Fréchet ChemNet Distance)

Distance between feature distributions of generated and reference sets.

$$\text{FCD} = \|\mu_g - \mu_r\|^2 + \text{Tr}(\Sigma_g + \Sigma_r - 2(\Sigma_g \Sigma_r)^{1/2})$$
