Variational Reinforcement Learning

Theoretical foundations of probabilistic policy optimization with uncertainty quantification for risk-aware financial decision making

Introduction

Variational Reinforcement Learning (VRL) extends traditional RL by incorporating probabilistic inference into the policy optimization process. Instead of learning deterministic action-value functions, VRL maintains distributions over policies and value functions, enabling principled uncertainty quantification crucial for financial applications.

Uncertainty Quantification

Explicit modeling of epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty in market predictions and trading decisions.

Risk-Aware Policies

Policies that account for uncertainty in position sizing and risk management.

Exploration-Exploitation

Principled balance using Thompson sampling and information-theoretic objectives.

Implementation

class VariationalActor(nn.Module): def __init__(self, state_dim, action_dim, hidden_dim=256): super().__init__() self.fc1 = nn.Linear(state_dim, hidden_dim) self.fc2 = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, hidden_dim) self.mean = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, action_dim) self.log_std = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, action_dim) def forward(self, state): x = F.relu(self.fc1(state)) x = F.relu(self.fc2(x)) mean = self.mean(x) log_std = self.log_std(x).clamp(-20, 2) std = log_std.exp() return Normal(mean, std) def sample_action(self, state): dist = self.forward(state) action = dist.rsample() # Reparameterization trick log_prob = dist.log_prob(action).sum(-1) return action, log_prob, dist.entropy()

Applications in Trading

  • Position Sizing: Uncertainty-aware position sizing that reduces exposure when model confidence is low
  • Portfolio Allocation: Mean-variance optimization with learned uncertainty estimates
  • Risk Management: Dynamic stop-loss and take-profit levels based on predicted volatility
  • Strategy Selection: Thompson sampling across multiple trading strategies
  • Market Regime Detection: Bayesian changepoint detection for regime-adaptive trading
