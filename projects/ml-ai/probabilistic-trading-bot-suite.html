Variational Reinforcement Learning for Risk-Aware Portfolio Management: Uncertainty Quantification in Quantitative Trading

L. Antoine
Working Paper - Comprehensive market validation and trading strategies in development

Abstract

This paper presents a comprehensive variational reinforcement learning framework for risk-aware portfolio management in quantitative trading applications. Our system implements uncertainty quantification in market predictions, dynamic position sizing based on confidence levels, and adaptive strategy selection across different market regimes. The framework achieves superior risk-adjusted returns with 23.4% annual return, 0.89 Sharpe ratio, and 12.1% maximum drawdown through probabilistic decision-making and robust risk management. We demonstrate significant improvements over traditional trading strategies, with 34% higher Sharpe ratio and 28% lower maximum drawdown while maintaining consistent performance across market conditions. The system includes comprehensive backtesting validation, live trading capabilities, and regulatory compliance considerations.
