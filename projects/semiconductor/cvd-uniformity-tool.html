Uniformity Metrics

Non-Uniformity Definitions

1σ (Standard Deviation): σ/μ × 100%

3σ (Three Sigma): 3σ/μ × 100%

Range: (Max - Min) / (2 × Mean) × 100%

Radial Uniformity: Variation from center to edge

Industry Specifications

  • Research/Development: σ/μ < 5%
  • Production (mature): σ/μ < 2%
  • Advanced nodes: σ/μ < 1%
  • Edge exclusion typically: 3-10 mm
