Double Gaussian PSF Formula

PSF(x,y) = A₁ × exp(-(x²+y²)/(2σ₁²)) + A₂ × exp(-(x²+y²)/(2σ₂²))

Where A₁, A₂ are amplitudes and σ₁, σ₂ are standard deviations
