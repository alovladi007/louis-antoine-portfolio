Contrast and NILS Formulas

Contrast = (I_max - I_min) / (I_max + I_min)
NILS = (I_max - I_min) / (I_max + I_min) × (2π/p) × (1/NA)

Where I_max/min are maximum/minimum intensities, p is pitch, and NA is numerical aperture
