Loss Tolerance Simulator

Model per-component optical loss and identify critical thresholds for entanglement preservation

Loss Channel Model

Optical loss is modeled as a beamsplitter mixing with vacuum:

Vlossy = η Videal + (1-η) Vvac

η = 10^(-loss_dB/10) per component

Critical threshold (~0.7 dB/layer):

  • Below threshold: Full entanglement preserved
  • At threshold: Marginal performance
  • Above threshold: Rapid degradation
