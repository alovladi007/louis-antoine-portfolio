2. PPG Heart Rate Estimation

Photoplethysmography (PPG) measures blood volume changes using light absorption. Green LEDs (~530nm) are preferred for wrist-worn devices due to penetration depth and motion artifact resistance.

4. Sleep Stage Classification

Sleep staging from wearables uses movement (actigraphy) and heart rate patterns to classify Awake, REM, Light, and Deep sleep stages.

Sleep Stages

Awake

High movement, variable HR

REM

Low movement, variable HR

Light (N1/N2)

Moderate HR, some movement

Deep (N3)

Low HR, minimal movement

Features for Sleep Staging

  • Activity Count: Integrated accelerometer magnitude per epoch (30s)
  • Heart Rate: Mean and variability within epoch
  • HRV Metrics: RMSSD (parasympathetic activity indicator)
  • Respiratory Rate: Derived from PPG or accelerometer
