# Silicon Photonics Transceiver - References

## Key Literature

### Silicon Photonics Fundamentals
1. **Reed, G. T., et al.** "Silicon optical modulators." *Nature Photonics* 4.8 (2010): 518-526.
   - Comprehensive review of silicon optical modulators
   - Covers carrier depletion, carrier injection, and thermal effects

2. **Chrostowski, L., & Hochberg, M.** "Silicon Photonics Design: From Devices to Systems." Cambridge University Press, 2015.
   - Complete textbook on silicon photonics design
   - Covers theory, simulation, and fabrication

3. **Bogaerts, W., et al.** "Silicon photonics circuit design: methods, tools and challenges." *Laser & Photonics Reviews* 12.4 (2018): 1700237.
   - Design methodologies for silicon photonic circuits
   - CAD tools and design flow

### Ring Modulators
4. **Xu, Q., et al.** "Micrometre-scale silicon electro-optic modulator." *Nature* 435.7040 (2005): 325-327.
   - First demonstration of silicon ring modulator
   - Carrier depletion mechanism

5. **Dong, P., et al.** "High-speed low-voltage single-drive push-pull silicon Mach-Zehnder modulators." *Optics Express* 20.6 (2012): 6163-6169.
   - High-speed modulator design
   - Push-pull configuration

6. **Timurdogan, E., et al.** "An ultralow power athermal silicon modulator." *Nature Communications* 5.1 (2014): 4008.
   - Low-power modulator design
   - Thermal stability

### Germanium Photodetectors
7. **Michel, J., et al.** "High-performance Ge-on-Si photodetectors." *Nature Photonics* 4.8 (2010): 527-534.
   - Ge photodetector on Si platform
   - High responsivity and bandwidth

8. **Assefa, S., et al.** "Reinventing germanium avalanche photodetector for nanophotonic on-chip optical interconnects." *Nature* 464.7285 (2010): 80-84.
   - Ge avalanche photodetector
   - High sensitivity

9. **Kang, Y., et al.** "Monolithic germanium/silicon avalanche photodiodes with 340 GHz gain-bandwidth product." *Nature Photonics* 3.1 (2009): 59-63.
   - High-speed Ge APD
   - Gain-bandwidth product

### System Integration
10. **Chen, L., et al.** "Demonstration of 12.5 Gb/s optical data transmission at 1.55 μm using silicon Mach-Zehnder modulator." *Optics Express* 15.1 (2007): 500-505.
    - System-level demonstration
    - Data transmission results

11. **Liu, A., et al.** "High-speed optical modulation based on carrier depletion in a silicon waveguide." *Optics Express* 15.2 (2007): 660-668.
    - High-speed modulation
    - Carrier depletion mechanism

12. **Liao, S., et al.** "36 GHz submicron silicon waveguide germanium photodetector." *Optics Express* 19.11 (2011): 10967-10972.
    - High-bandwidth Ge PD
    - Submicron waveguide integration

### Packaging and Integration
13. **Chen, H., et al.** "25 Gbps low-voltage waveguide Si-Ge avalanche photodetector." *Optics Express* 24.21 (2016): 24575-24582.
    - High-speed Ge APD
    - Low-voltage operation

14. **Srinivasan, S. A., et al.** "56 Gb/s germanium waveguide electro-absorption modulator." *Journal of Lightwave Technology* 34.2 (2016): 419-424.
    - High-speed Ge modulator
    - Waveguide integration

15. **Ding, R., et al.** "Low-loss and broadband non-reciprocal silicon photonic device." *Optics Express* 22.4 (2014): 4137-4143.
    - Non-reciprocal devices
    - Low-loss design

### Simulation and Modeling
16. **Bogaerts, W., et al.** "Efficient coupling between dielectric and plasmonic waveguides." *Optics Express* 15.4 (2007): 2190-2204.
    - Coupling simulation
    - FDTD modeling

17. **Reed, G. T., et al.** "Silicon optical modulators." *Materials Today* 13.7-8 (2010): 26-33.
    - Modulator modeling
    - Performance optimization

18. **Dumon, P., et al.** "Compact wavelength-selective functions in silicon-on-insulator photonic wires." *IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Quantum Electronics* 12.6 (2006): 1394-1401.
    - Wavelength-selective devices
    - Compact design

### Process Technology
19. **Soref, R.** "The past, present, and future of silicon photonics." *IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Quantum Electronics* 12.6 (2006): 1678-1687.
    - Historical perspective
    - Future directions

20. **Pavesi, L., & Lockwood, D. J.** "Silicon Photonics." Springer, 2004.
    - Comprehensive silicon photonics reference
    - Theory and applications

### Standards and Specifications

    - Ethernet specifications
