Mathematical Analysis

Langmuir Adsorption Model

$$\theta = \frac{K P}{1 + K P}$$

Where θ is the surface coverage, K is the equilibrium constant, and P is the precursor pressure.

Reaction Rate Expression

$$r = k \cdot \theta_A \cdot \theta_B$$

Where r is the reaction rate, k is the rate constant, and θ_A, θ_B are the surface coverages of reactants.
