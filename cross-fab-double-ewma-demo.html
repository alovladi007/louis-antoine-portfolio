Double-EWMA Mathematical Model

The Double-EWMA control algorithm uses two exponentially weighted moving averages:

$$E_1(t) = \lambda_1 \cdot Y(t) + (1-\lambda_1) \cdot E_1(t-1)$$

$$E_2(t) = \lambda_2 \cdot E_1(t) + (1-\lambda_2) \cdot E_2(t-1)$$

$$U(t) = U(t-1) + \frac{E_1(t) - E_2(t)}{1-\lambda_2}$$

Where λ₁ and λ₂ are smoothing parameters, Y(t) is the process output, E₁(t) and E₂(t) are the EWMA statistics, and U(t) is the control action.
