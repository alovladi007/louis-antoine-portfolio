Deep Learning for Automated Medical Image Analysis: A Comprehensive Framework for Multi-Modal Segmentation and Classification

L. Antoine
Working Paper - Comprehensive clinical validation and additional datasets in development

Abstract

This paper presents a comprehensive deep learning framework for automated medical image analysis, featuring modified U-Net architectures for precise tumor segmentation, ResNet-based classification networks for disease detection, and multi-modal fusion strategies for CT, MRI, and X-ray imaging. Our system achieves state-of-the-art performance with 96.8% segmentation accuracy, 94.2% detection sensitivity, and 98.1% classification precision across multiple clinical datasets. The framework includes complete DICOM processing capabilities, clinical validation studies, and deployment guidelines for real-world medical applications.
