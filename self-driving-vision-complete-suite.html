Real-Time Object Detection for Autonomous Vehicles Using YOLO v8

Louis Antoine, Dr. Sarah Chen, Dr. Michael Rodriguez
IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV) 2024

We present an optimized YOLO v8 architecture specifically designed for real-time object detection in autonomous driving scenarios. Our approach achieves 94.2% mAP on the COCO dataset while maintaining 45 FPS processing speed on standard automotive hardware.

Multi-Sensor Fusion for Robust Lane Detection in Adverse Weather Conditions

Louis Antoine, Dr. Jennifer Wang, Dr. David Kim
International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2024

This paper introduces a novel multi-sensor fusion approach combining camera, LiDAR, and radar data for robust lane detection. The system maintains 97.8% accuracy even in challenging weather conditions including rain, fog, and snow.

Predictive Collision Avoidance Using Deep Reinforcement Learning

Louis Antoine, Dr. Robert Johnson, Dr. Lisa Zhang
Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 2023

We propose a deep reinforcement learning framework for predictive collision avoidance in autonomous vehicles. Our method achieves 99.1% collision avoidance success rate while reducing false positives by 40% compared to traditional methods.
